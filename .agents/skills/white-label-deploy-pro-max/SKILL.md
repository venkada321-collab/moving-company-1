This authoritative operational protocol defines an end-to-end, deterministic AI framework for engineering static standard website templates into multi-tenant template engines, extracting target brand intelligence, and deploying production-grade bespoke web applications on Cloudflare Pages and GitHub.

## 🏛️ The 6-Pillar & Atomic Matrix White-Labeling Architecture

Every white-labeled application generated by this engine MUST enforce our 6 core pillars of modular site customization, integrated with our combinatorial Atomic Matrix generator:

### 1. Robust Theming Engine (Design Tokens & Niche-Restricted Bounded Uniqueness)
All visual styles are driven by dynamic configuration tokens (`src/config/theme.ts`) rather than hardcoded component utility classes:
* **Niche-Restricted Color Boundaries:** To prevent inappropriate color aesthetics (e.g., generating high-saturation neon magentas or electric cyber-greens fit for a gaming or esports portal when engineering corporate platforms), the engine enforces strict industry color psychology boundaries (`NICHE_PALETTE_REGISTRIES` in `scripts/color_spectrum_engine.cjs`). For professional niches like Relocation, Logistics, Legal, or Medical, acceptable anchor spectrums are curated specifically to build authority, trust, and structural reliability (such as Executive Cobalts, Maritime Cyans, Royal Indigos, Safety Ambers, Slate Graphites, and Eco-Spruces).
* **Bounded Within-Niche HSL Chromatic Shifting:** To guarantee complete chromatic differentiation across dozens of competitor clones without breaking out of professional niche psychology, the engine computes deterministic HSL micro-variations ($\Delta\text{Hue} \in [-12^\circ, +12^\circ]$, $\Delta\text{Saturation} \in [-10\%, +10\%]$) from the brand's cryptographic fingerprint. This ensures that two websites sharing an Executive Cobalt theme emit mathematically distinct hex codes (`#1E3A8A` vs `#233B90`), passing perceptual Delta-E distance testing while maintaining 100% niche appropriateness!
* **Color Palettes & Triads:** Map Primary (Major), Secondary (Minor), and Tertiary (Accent) colors, plus Semantic colors (success/emerald, warning/amber, error/rose) and background shades (`bgPage`, `bgSection`, `bgCardAlt`, `bgFooter`).
* **Typography Systems:** Configurable heading & body font pairings (`Montserrat` + `Inter` for modern tech, `Space Grotesk` + `DM Sans` for geometric luxury, `Plus Jakarta Sans` + `Manrope` for clean modern).
* **Border Radii & Shadows:** Controllable radii (`rounded-none` for stark corporate, `rounded-3xl`/`rounded-full` for modern pills) and drop shadow intensity (`shadow-sm`, `shadow-2xl`).

### 2. Modular Component Architecture & DOM Scrambling
The template utilizes a library of stackable, toggleable, and re-orderable conversion funnel components:
* **Autonomous Section Visibility Toggles:** The engine discovers and extracts all content blocks into a dynamic `sectionsEnabled` record and `sectionOrder` array in `src/config/layout.ts`.
* **DOM Section Scrambling (Conversion Funnel Shuffling):** Dynamically mutates page structure and section ordering based on brand identity (e.g. Standard Executive Funnel vs. Luxury Trust-First Funnel with Verified Reviews directly below Hero vs. Suburban Logistics Funnel).
* **Structural Layout Variants:** Switches macro component layouts (e.g. Hero split calculator vs. centered hero text vs. compact banner).

### 3. The Atomic Matrix Generator (759,375 Permutations per Component)
To eliminate structural DOM repetition across multi-tenant fleets, the engine integrates an automated 5-dimensional Atomic Matrix generator (`scripts/atomic_matrix_engine.cjs`). Each dimension contains exactly 15 production-ready choices ($15^5 = 759,375$ unique structural layout combinations per component):
* **Dimension 1 (Container Topologies):** Symmetrical Air Grids, Asymmetric Bento Boxes, Horizontal Assurance Ribbons, Stacked Pills, Masonry Layouts, Split-Screen Matrices.
* **Dimension 2 (Surface Geometries & Depth):** Flat Minimalism, Frosted Glassmorphism, Heavy Brutalist Outlines, Neomorphic Soft Glow, Dual-Tone Floating Bins.
* **Dimension 3 (Iconography & Accent Strategies):** Minimal line icons, solid duotone shields, glowing neon anchors, metallic badges.
* **Dimension 4 (Typography & Hierarchy Scales):** Compact utility text, oversized editorial headlines, mono-spaced data metrics, tracking-widened tags.
* **Dimension 5 (Interactive Motion Cadences):** Snappy tech translations, luxury buttery easing, bouncy energetic scale lifts, elastic staggered reveals.

### 4. Brand-Specific Imagery, Iconography & Shapes
* **Zero-CORS Multi-Strategy Asset Retrieval:** Automatically harvests and localizes authentic high-resolution logos, favicons, and hero assets from target domains via `retrieve_logo.cjs`.
* **Shape Dividers & Overlays:** Customizable section transitions (`soft-wave`, `diagonal-cut`, `rounded-curve`, `flat`).

### 5. Atmospheric Polarization (Theme & Color Canvas)
* **Dynamic Canvas Assignment:** Transforms sites between Ultra-Crisp Light Mode (`#FFFFFF`), Midnight Obsidian Executive Dark Mode (`#09090B` with rose/indigo glow), and Warm Editorial Ivory Mode (`#FDFBF7` with stone/slate magazine typography).
* **Palette-Aware Backgrounds:** Matches atmospheric mode directly to the brand's extracted primary colors so each site feels completely custom built.

### 6. Distinct Content & Microcopy Matrix
* **Unique Value Propositions (UVPs):** Tailored hero headers, regional ranking claims, and niche value statements.
* **Personality-Driven Microcopy & Legal Rigor:** Rotates conversational CTA text, WSIB registration IDs, provincial transit numbers, insurance policies ($1M vs $5M COI), and verified review counts across all 10 modules.

## 📋 Operational Execution Workflow

### Phase 0: Master Template Engine Engineering (Refactoring Normal Templates)
When tasked with starting from a standard, static website template that lacks modular customization architecture, the agent **MUST** execute autonomous inspection and engineer the template into an automated white-label engine before attempting brand extraction or site cloning:

1. **Extract Global Design Tokens (`src/config/theme.ts`, `theme-tokens.css` & `src/index.css`):**
   - Audit all component source files to identify hardcoded color hex codes, typography selections, border radii (e.g., `rounded-3xl`), elevation drop shadows, and transition timings.
   - Extract these values into a centralized `THEME` export in `theme.ts` and set up zero-runtime RGB CSS variable injection (`:root { --primary-500: ... }`) in `src/config/theme-tokens.css` so brand colors and typography can be transformed programmatically.
2. **Autonomous Niche Inference & Dynamic Section Discovery (`src/config/layout.ts`):**
   - Without requiring manual industry toggles or pre-set section lists, inspect the base template's existing JSX/HTML structure, content headings, and UI widgets to understand its underlying industry domain and service model (whether professional services, SaaS, healthcare, construction, e-commerce, or hospitality).
   - Automatically discover every physical section block within the template (e.g., hero banner, service matrices, calculators/estimators, pricing tables, case studies, FAQ accordions, newsletter opt-ins, or contact forms).
   - Extract each discovered section into an independent modular React component and synthesize a custom `SectionId` union type and boolean visibility matrix (`sectionsEnabled`) in `src/config/layout.ts` tailored precisely to the discovered architectural blocks of that specific template.
3. **Centralize Copywriting & Microcopy Registries (`src/config/microcopy.ts` & `src/config/brand.ts`):**
   - Isolate company identities, addresses, legal disclaimers, button call-to-action text, form placeholder strings, and value claims into configuration modules aligned with the discovered niche.
4. **Implement Shape Divider & Geometry Overlays (`src/components/SectionDivider.tsx`):**
   - Build customizable decorative SVG section boundaries (`soft-wave`, `diagonal-cut`, `rounded-curve`, `flat`) between structural page blocks.
5. **Develop Automated Substitution Automation (`scripts/populate_template.cjs`):**
   - Write a Node automation script that ingests target JSON onboarding profiles and programmatically modifies `brand.ts`, `theme.ts`, `theme-tokens.css`, `layout.ts`, and `microcopy.ts` to transform the application without manual code editing.

### Phase 1: Target Website Intelligence Extraction & Asset Profiling

#### Automated Brand Intelligence Extraction (`scripts/extract_brand.cjs`)
This script uses Playwright to deep-scrape a live target website and automatically build a complete brand profile. It extracts:
- **Identity:** Company name (from og:title, page title, logo alt text, h1) with graphic filename filtering (purging string artifacts like `Logo2.png Inc.`), legal corporate name, short name
- **Contact Directory:** Phone numbers (North American format regex + `tel:` links), email addresses (`mailto:` links + body regex), physical address (Canadian postal code patterns)
- **3-Color Brand Palette Triads:** Computes HSL values from all interactive and branded DOM elements (buttons, headers, nav, hero), scores chromatic intensity, and assigns an architectural color triad consisting of **Primary**, **Secondary**, and **Tertiary** palettes to guarantee harmonious contrast across backgrounds and UI widgets
- **Typography:** Discovers heading and body font families from computed styles, maps to our supported font library
- **Hero Content:** Extracts h1 tagline, subtitle paragraph, and CTA button text
- **Social Media:** Discovers Facebook, Instagram, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Yelp, Google links
- **Favicon & Logo URLs:** Multi-selector priority discovery from the DOM with automated protocol (`https://`, `http://`) and subdomain (`www.`) fallback retry loops to guarantee zero-CORS logo resolution even when target SSL or DNS redirection is misconfigured. When semantic tags (`<header>`, `<nav>`) or standard `"logo"` filenames/alt-text are absent (common on page builders like GoHighLevel, ClickFunnels, Elementor, and Wix), the engine executes **Geometric Top-of-Fold Visual Discovery**, checking browser rendering coordinates (`rect.top < 300` and `rect.height` between `25px` and `180px`) to locate and extract the authentic brand header graphic!
- **Authoritative Existing Site Reference Screenshot & Brand Vibes:** Automatically employs Playwright during extraction to save a full-page reference screenshot (`./output/references/<slug>-existing-reference.png`) of the existing target website (when accessible). From this live DOM baseline, the engine computes **Color Cues**, structural density, and **Brand Vibes** (e.g. Content-Dense Classic vs. Minimalist Corporate), setting up an automated **Before vs. Pro-Max Improvement Category Comparison Report** after deployment!

```powershell
# Single website extraction
node scripts/extract_brand.cjs https://getmovers.ca
node scripts/extract_brand.cjs uniquemovers.ca ./profiles/unique-movers.json
```

#### Zero-CORS Logo Retrieval & Logo Chromatic Primary Color Extraction (`scripts/retrieve_logo.cjs`)
Multi-strategy asset downloader that bypasses hotlink blocks, WAF firewalls, CDN restrictions, and CORS policies using an advanced geometric cascade:
1. **Direct DOM & Geometric Top-of-Fold Discovery:** Identifies logo source URLs via semantic selectors and layout bounding box geometry (`rect.top < 300px`), followed by Direct HTTP fetch with Chrome UA + Sec-Fetch headers.
2. **Playwright full browser navigation** (handles Cloudflare-protected, JS-required, Jetpack CDN)
3. **Playwright element screenshot** (handles inline SVG logos, CSS-background logos, data: URIs)
4. **SVG symbol badge generation** (guaranteed fallback — never fails)

* **🎨 Unconditional Logo Chromatic Primary Color Analysis:** Whenever `retrieve_logo.cjs --profile` downloads an authentic brand graphic into `./public/assets/brand/logo.png`, the script automatically renders the image onto a virtual Playwright canvas, samples the raw RGB/HSL alpha pixel distributions across the logo, identifies the dominant chromatic brand tone (e.g., emerald, blue, indigo, red, amber, or monochrome slate), and sets it directly as the website's authoritative Primary Brand Color Palette (`profile.theme.primary = logoPalette`)! The primary brand logo is the ultimate source of truth for the landing page color scheme, operating within the strict boundaries of our **Niche-Restricted Color Spectrum Engine**.

```powershell
# Direct URL download
node scripts/retrieve_logo.cjs https://getmovers.ca/img/logo.png ./public/assets/brand/
# Auto-discover and download from a live website
node scripts/retrieve_logo.cjs --discover https://uniquemovers.ca ./public/assets/brand/
# Process a target-profile.json (downloads all assets & extracts logo primary color)
node scripts/retrieve_logo.cjs --profile target-profile.json
```

### Phase 3: UI/UX Pro Max Aesthetic & Accessibility Enforcement
All customized builds **MUST** pass strict design quality controls:
* **Contrast Compliance (WCAG AA):** Minimum 4.5:1 text-to-background contrast ratio across all light/dark elements.
* **Touch Targets (WCAG 2.5.5):** All interactive elements minimum **44x44px** touch target area (`min-h-[44px] py-3.5 px-6`).
* **Keyboard Focus Indicators:** Visible focus rings (`focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-black`).
* **Zero Generic Defaults:** High-impact visual hierarchy with zero unstyled placeholders.
* **Light Mode Default Principle:** The master Dark Mode toggle is dynamically evaluated in `src/App.tsx` (Lines 147-151), injecting the `dark` class onto the root container based on the selected Theme Canvas. Because of this, **components MUST follow light mode styles by default** (e.g., `bg-white`, `text-neutral-900`), and must exclusively use the `dark:` prefix for dark mode styles (e.g., `dark:bg-neutral-800`). Hardcoding dark backgrounds without the `dark:` prefix fundamentally breaks the master toggle.

### Phase 6: Batch Production & Multi-Site Fleet Deployment
When the Master Template Engine architecture is already built and the task requires generating **multi-site fleets** (e.g., producing 20 unique branded websites simultaneously from a JSON list of existing competitors or domain names), the agent can skip directly to batch execution:

#### Supported Input Modes:
1. **Mode A: Plain Domain Strings List (Agent Extraction Loop)**
   - **Input Format:** `["atlasrelocation.ca", "titanexpress.ca", "elitevanlines.com"]`
   - **Agent Protocol:** When given raw domain strings, the agent SHOULD first iterate over the array using web reading/browser tools to extract brand colors, taglines, and contact directories, writing an enriched JSON profile array before batch cloning. Alternatively, passing raw domain strings directly to the batch engine will trigger automated baseline profile generation.
2. **Mode B: Pre-Extracted Profile JSON Array**
   - **Input Format:** Array of full 5-Pillar target profile objects (see `examples/batch-targets.sample.json`).

#### Execution Commands:
1. **Local Batch Cloning & Verification (No Deployment):**
   ```powershell

   ```
   *Note: This script will aggregate the fully-resolved `profile` configurations for the entire fleet and output them to `output/batch_variants_dump.json`. You can use this file for external verification or AI-driven copy enhancement workflows.*
   * *What happens:* Automatically creates isolated site builds under `./output/<brand-slug>/`, runs `populate_template.cjs` per brand, and executes Vite compiler testing across all builds with error isolation and resilience.

2. **Automated Live Fleet Deployment (GitHub + Cloudflare):**
   ```powershell
   node scripts/batch_clone.cjs <path_to_batch.json> --deploy
   ```
   * *What happens:* After verification, initializes Git inside each isolated directory in `./output/<brand-slug>/`, provisions remote repositories, triggers Cloudflare Edge production deployments, and renders a unified tabular summary report of all production live URLs.

3. **Structural Variant Verification (Pre-Deployment):**
   Before executing the batch generator, the agent MUST explicitly inspect the `options` arrays defined inside `batch_clone.cjs` (e.g., `heroOptions`, `navOptions`, `howOptions`) and verify that every string explicitly maps to a reachable internal component route in `App.tsx` or the respective domain component (e.g. `HowItWorks.tsx`). #### 🛡️ Deterministic Cryptographic Fingerprinting & Algorithmic Differentiation (Anti-Cloning Guarantee)
To ensure that websites generated from the same template engine exhibit massive visual and structural divergence while maintaining deterministic build stability across deployments, the engine executes **Cryptographic SHA-256 Brand Fingerprinting** (`hashIdx`):
1. **Deterministic Cryptographic Hash Index:** Instead of relying on sequential array index numbers (`i % length`), `batch_clone.cjs` and `populate_template.cjs` compute a 32-bit cryptographic hash integer from the target brand slug or domain (`crypto.createHash('sha256').update(slug).digest('hex')`).
   * *Why this is essential:* It guarantees that any single brand builds with **100% reproducible layout, typography, and atmosphere consistency** across redeployments and automated CI/CD builds, while ensuring that any two different brand names emerge with wildly divergent layouts—even when run as single-item batch jobs!
2. **Atomic Matrix Component Injection:** The cryptographic fingerprint feeds directly into `generateAtomicBlueprint(brandSlug, moduleKey)`, synthesizing custom Tailored utility class combinations across all 5 dimensions ($759,375$ permutations) for core conversion components (Services, Routes, Blog, Supplies, Process).
3. **The 3-Pillar UI/UX Pro Max Uniqueness Transformations:**
   * **Atmospheric Polarization:** Assigns Light Mode, Obsidian Midnight Dark Mode, or Warm Editorial Ivory Mode tailored to the brand's HSL primary palette.
   * **DOM Section Scrambling:** Re-orders HTML document conversion funnels (Trust-First vs. Logistics-First vs. Standard Executive).
   * **Algorithmic Grids & Proportions:** Adjusts grid ratios (`grid-cols-[2fr_3fr]` vs `grid-cols-4`) and breathing room (`gap-3` vs `gap-12`).
4. **Macro UI Personality Profiles & Compliance Footers:** Switches across modern-standard, brutalist, soft-glassmorphic, and luxury-minimalist themes, with custom regulatory compliance footer ribbons (WSIB numbers, Transport Canada credentials, and legal disclaimers) completely eliminating generic template signatures.
5. **🛡️ Pre-Build Statistical Perceptual Uniqueness & Hero Variation Engine:** Instead of waiting until after compilation to discover repetition, `batch_clone.cjs` continuously inspects assigned Atomic Matrix vectors *before* touching disk or calling Vite build! It categorizes component elements into **High-Perception Visual Drivers** (Hero Canvas, Navigation Architecture, UI Personality Profile, Primary Palette Triad) versus **Secondary Structural Elements** (FAQ accordions, footer ribbons, legal tables). The engine enforces a strict **7-8 Site Sliding Variation Rule**: high-perception visual features (such as Hero Canvases and primary color anchors) are mathematically guaranteed never to repeat within any window of 7 to 8 consecutive websites in a batch. If a candidate brand's assigned matrix collides with a recent sibling, the engine automatically mutates the cryptographic seed to spin out a guaranteed perceptually unique visual presentation instantly!

* *Automated Behavior:* When processing target profiles or domain lists, `batch_clone.cjs` directly injects the brand's cryptographic hash into `populate_template.cjs`, activating the Atomic Matrix, Pre-Build Statistical Variation check, and 6-Pillar substitution engine automatically.
